    About Abi's Drapes & Makeup

    Bringing the art of South Indian beauty to Charlotte, North Carolina

    Meet Abi

    Hello! I'm Abi, the founder of Abi's Drapes & Makeup. While I'm currently pursuing my degree in software engineering, my true passion has always been the art of makeup and traditional South Indian beauty.

    Growing up immersed in South Indian culture, I developed a deep appreciation for the intricate art of bridal makeup and the elegant tradition of saree draping. What started as helping family and friends has blossomed into a fulfilling journey of making brides feel absolutely beautiful on their special day.

    I specialize in authentic South Indian bridal makeup that honors traditional techniques while incorporating modern makeup artistry. From perfectly draped Kanjivaram sarees to stunning bridal transformations, I pour my heart into every service I provide.

    Based in Charlotte, North Carolina, I'm proud to bring these cherished traditions to our community. Whether you're a bride preparing for your big day or attending a special event, I'm here to help you look and feel your absolute best.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How far in advance should I book? +
    For bridal services, we recommend booking 3–6 months in advance. For other services, 2–4 weeks notice is typically sufficient.
    Do you travel for appointments? +
    Yes! We provide on-location services throughout Charlotte and surrounding areas. A travel fee of $30 applies for locations outside Charlotte city limits.
    What is your cancellation policy? +
    We require 48 hours notice for cancellations. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before your appointment may be subject to a cancellation fee.
    Do you provide trials before the wedding? +
    Yes! We highly recommend a trial session before your wedding day. Trial sessions are $120 and allow us to perfect your look.
