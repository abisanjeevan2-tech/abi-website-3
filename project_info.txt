================================================================================
GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

EASIEST WAY (30 seconds):
1. Open index.html in your browser
2. Done! Site works immediately.

WITH FIREBASE (20 minutes):
1. Follow QUICKSTART.md or docs/FIREBASE_SETUP.md
2. Configure Firebase project
3. Update index.html with config
4. Test locally
5. Deploy

FULL CUSTOMIZATION:
1. Read docs/CUSTOMIZATION.md
2. Update branding and content
3. Add your images
4. Test thoroughly
5. Deploy using docs/DEPLOYMENT.md
